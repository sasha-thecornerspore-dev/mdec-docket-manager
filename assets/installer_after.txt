MDEC Docket Manager is installed.

FIRST RUN

1. Open the app from your Desktop or Start Menu.
2. Settings -> Cases -> add your case number (as printed, with dashes) and
   choose a folder for its documents.
3. Click "Open portal window" and sign in to the Maryland Judiciary portal
   yourself. The app never handles your password unless you explicitly turn on
   managed login.
4. Click "Check now".

The first check on a large docket takes a while - roughly 20 minutes per 1,000
documents - because downloads are paced to stay under the portal's throttle.
Later checks only fetch what is new, and take seconds.

A ONE-TIME DOWNLOAD

The first check also downloads a private copy of Chromium (about 130 MB) that
the app drives. The app tells you when this is happening. It is separate from
your everyday browser and keeps its own signed-in session.

THINGS WORTH KNOWING

- Closing the window does NOT quit the app. The service keeps running so
  scheduled checks still happen. Clicking the icon again reopens the window.
  To stop it: Settings -> Quit app.
- Your documents, notes, and settings live outside this folder, so upgrading or
  uninstalling never touches them.
- Analysis features are informational only and are NOT legal advice. Verify
  every date and deadline against the filings themselves.

Documentation is in the docs folder, and on GitHub:
